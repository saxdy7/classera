# How to Create Communities - Quick Guide

## For Mentors:

### Step 1: Navigate to Communities
1. Log in as a **Mentor**
2. Look at the **left sidebar** (it will expand when you hover over it)
3. Click on the **"Communities"** icon (👥 UsersRound icon)
   - It's the 6th item in the sidebar
   - Path: `/dashboard/mentor/communities`

### Step 2: Create Community Button
Once you're on the Communities page, you'll see:
- A **header** with "Communities" title
- A **"Create Community"** button in the top-right corner (purple/indigo gradient button with a Plus icon)
- Stats showing your communities count

### Step 3: Fill the Form
Click "Create Community" and you'll be taken to:
- Path: `/dashboard/mentor/communities/create`
- Fill in:
  - Community Name (required)
  - Description (required)
  - Avatar (optional)
  - Active/Inactive toggle
  - Approval settings

### Step 4: Submit
- Click "Create Community" button at the bottom
- You'll be redirected back to the communities list
- Your new community will appear in the grid

## Troubleshooting:

### Can't see the sidebar?
- Make sure you're on desktop (sidebar is hidden on mobile)
- The sidebar is on the left side and expands when you hover
- It's a floating panel with rounded corners

### Can't see Communities option?
- Verify you're logged in as a **Mentor** (not Student)
- Check the URL: should be `/dashboard/mentor`
- Communities is the 6th item in the sidebar

### Button not appearing?
- Make sure you're on the Communities page (`/dashboard/mentor/communities`)
- The button is in the header section, top-right
- It says "Create Community" with a Plus (+) icon
